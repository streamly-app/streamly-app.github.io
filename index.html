Downloads

Take it offline. Watch anywhere.

Save movies and episodes for the plane, the car, the cabin. Streamly's smart downloader manages your storage, queues by Wi-Fi, and keeps you a few episodes ahead automatically.

  • Wi-Fi only by default — never blow through your cellular plan by accident
  • Smart Downloads — keeps a configurable number of unwatched episodes ready
  • Storage cap — set a hard ceiling (default 10 GB), oldest watched item evicts first
  • Concurrent downloads — tune how many run in parallel based on your connection
Playlists

Bring any IPTV. In two taps.

Cross-device sync

Pause on iPhone. Resume on Apple TV.

Sign in once. Your watch progress, favorites, and playlists follow you across every device you sign into. Encrypted via Firebase, scoped to your account, never sold.

  • Continue Watching syncs across iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Mac
  • Favorites and watch history move with your account (Pro)
  • Sign in with Apple — one tap, no passwords
  • Stays local-only on Free; sync activates with Pro

⚡

Hybrid playback engine

VLC for live TV (handles weird codecs like HEVC-over-TS). Native AVPlayer for everything else. Automatic fallback on failure. You never see "codec not supported."

📱

Native on every screen

Built specifically for iOS, iPadOS, tvOS, and macOS in SwiftUI. Focus-engine first on Apple TV. Pixel-perfect on iPhone. Mouse + keyboard on Mac. No web-app port.

🛡️

Your data, your device

Streamly stores no streams. Your IPTV credentials live in the iOS Keychain. Watch history is local-only on Free; private Firestore sync on Pro. No ads. No tracking.
