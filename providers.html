Send your users straight into Streamly

If you run a TV service or your own media server, one link or QR code opens Streamly on Add Playlist with your server address already filled in. Your users type their own username and password, and they are watching.

Enter an address that starts with http:// or https:// and has no username or password in it.

Everything on this page is worked out in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us.

How it works for your users

  1. They open your link or scan your code. A short page shows your name and address, with buttons to get Streamly.
  2. They tap Open in Streamly. The app opens on Add Playlist with your address filled in.
  3. They type the username and password you gave them and save. That is all.

Good to know

  • No passwords in links. A link carries an address and a name, never a login. Streamly ignores any username or password put into one.
  • Nothing happens automatically. The app shows the address and waits for the user. It does not save or connect by itself.
  • Phones, tablets and Mac. The link opens Streamly on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. On Apple TV and Android TV users add the address with the remote.
  • Streamly is only a player. It does not include, sell or host channels, and it does not list, endorse or promote any service. This link is for services and servers whose content you have the right to offer.
  • Free. There is nothing to sign up for and no fee. Streamly is free to download; your users can use it with your playlist at no cost.

Running your own server?

The same link works for home setups. If your TVHeadend, Jellyfin, Channels or tuner software publishes an M3U address or an Xtream-style login, make a link once and send it to your family.
